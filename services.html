Private-Label Production

In-house

Men's shirts made to your tech pack and measurement chart, with your labels, trims and packaging. Start with one style and scale to full collections, at 8,000–20,000 units a month.

Formal and dress / Casual / Oxford, poplin and twill / Flannel / Linen / Denim and overshirts

In-house

Sampling

Samples made to your tech pack before bulk, so fit, construction and trims are approved before production starts.

Samples to your tech pack / Fit and construction review / Approval before bulk

Fabric and Trim Sourcing

On request

Supply your own fabric, or we source fabric and trims to your specification and run the order from there.

Fabric to your specification / Interlinings / Buttons and trims

Cutting

Through partners

Send pre-cut panels ready for the line, or send fabric and we arrange cutting with partner cutting rooms.

Cutting through partner cutting rooms / Pre-cut panels checked on arrival

Make and Finishing

In-house

Sewing, assembly and finishing on Juki, Brother and Jack lines, to the seam and stitch spec in your tech pack.

Collars, cuffs, yokes and plackets / Sleeves and assembly / Buttonholes and buttons / Thread trimming / Collar shaping / Steam pressing

Quality Control

In-house

Every order runs under our ISO 9001 quality management system, with checks from intake to dispatch.

Kit check on intake / Inline checks at each operation / Measurements against your size chart / Final inspection before packing

Labelling and Packing

In-house

Your shirts leave the factory retail-ready, labelled and packed to your packing list.

Brand and care labels / Hang tags / Folding on board / Polybags and boxes / Barcodes and size stickers / Cartons to your packing list and size ratio

Embroidery and Washing

Through partners

When a style needs embroidery, a garment wash or a special finish, we run it through partners we work with regularly.

Embroidery / Garment washing / Special finishes

Collection and Delivery

Tirana or Durrës port

Orders are collected from the factory in Tirana or delivered to Durrës port. Onward freight is arranged by you; for special requirements we can help through our contacts.

Collection from the factory, Tirana / Delivery to Durrës port
