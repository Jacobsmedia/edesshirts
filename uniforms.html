How a Tender Works With Us

1
of 5 steps

  1. Share the tender documents

    Send the tender reference, technical specification, drawings, quantities per size and the delivery schedule.

  2. Technical review and costing

    We review construction and trims, agree the fabric route (supplied by you or sourced to the required standard) and prepare costing for your bid.

  3. Samples for approval

    We produce samples to specification for your submission or for the contracting authority's approval and sealing.

  4. Production and inspection

    Bulk production under our ISO 9001 system, with inline checks and final inspection against the sealed sample.

  5. Delivery to schedule

    Labelled and packed to the contract's packing and labelling rules, then collected from the factory or delivered to Durrës port, in one shipment or by call-off.

FAQ

Can you provide quality certification for the tender dossier?

Yes. Production runs under an ISO 9001 certified quality management system, and the certificate can be included in your tender submission.

What monthly production capacity can you commit to a uniform contract?

8,000–20,000 shirts a month. Volumes for a specific contract are confirmed against its quantities and delivery schedule.

What workforce will run the contract?

A team of 70 people, 90% of them women, in a women-led company.

What is your track record in shirt construction?

We have manufactured shirts since 1998, with more than 30 years of collective expertise in shirt construction.

Do you work with contractor-supplied fabric, or source to the tender specification?

Both. Fabric can be supplied by the contractor, or we source it to the specification in the tender on request.

Can you produce submission and approval samples?

Yes. Samples are made to the technical specification, for tender submission and for approval before bulk production.

Where is production located, and where can you deliver?

Tirana, Albania. Orders are collected from the factory or delivered to Durrës port. Onward freight is arranged by you; for special requirements we can help through our contacts.

What are your minimum order quantity (MOQ) and lead time?

Both are confirmed per tender, based on the contract quantities and the delivery schedule.
