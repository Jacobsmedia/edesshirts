How EDES handles the details you send us through this website.

What We Collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, company or organisation, email address, phone number if you give one, the details of your enquiry (such as product type, quantities, fabric, delivery date or tender reference) and your message.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data. Please do not include it in your message.

Why We Use It

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out the order. This is a step you ask us to take before a contract, and it is in our legitimate interest to answer business enquiries.

We do not add you to a mailing list, and we never sell your details.

Who Sees It

Only EDES staff who handle enquiries read what you send. A few service providers process it on our behalf so the website and email can work:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers the form to our inbox
  • hCaptcha, which checks that the form is sent by a person, not a bot
  • Our email provider, which stores the emails we receive
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website

These providers may process data outside Albania and the European Union, including in the United States.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If an enquiry becomes an order, we keep order records for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

Cookies and Statistics

Our website does not set cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you across other websites.

On the contact page, hCaptcha may store data in your browser to run its check. Our fonts are stored on our own website. The animation library is loaded from cdnjs, a service run by Cloudflare, which receives your IP address in order to deliver the file.

Your Rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, send it to you in a portable format, or stop using it. Write to info@edesshirts.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to Albania's Information and Data Protection Commissioner, or, if you live in the European Union, to your local data protection authority, such as the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy.
