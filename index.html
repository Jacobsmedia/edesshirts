Production Process

1
of 5 steps

  1. Kit intake and check

    Pre-cut panels or fabric received and checked: piece counts per bundle, shade consistency and size labelling. Cutting arranged with partner cutting rooms when needed.

  2. Sewing and assembly

    Collars, cuffs, yokes, plackets and sleeves made and assembled on Juki and Brother lines, to the seam and stitch spec in your tech pack.

  3. Inline QC

    Checks at each operation for stitch quality, seam alignment and symmetry, with measurements taken against your size chart.

  4. Finishing

    Buttonholing, button sewing, labels and care labels, thread trimming, collar shaping and steam pressing.

  5. Final inspection and packing

    Final inspection, then folded, polybagged and cartoned to your packing list and size ratio, ready for dispatch.
