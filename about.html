Our Story

EDES started in 1998 as a family business in Tirana, making men's shirts. Today it is a women-led company that still makes one thing, for other people's brands.

From those beginnings we have grown into a cut-make-trim (CMT) manufacturer known for tight project management and attention to detail. Clients supply the fabric, usually as pre-cut panels; we run make, trim, finishing, inspection and packing in-house. When a client needs it, we source fabric or arrange cutting with partner cutting rooms.

Private label is the core of our business. Every order is built to the client's tech pack, with their labels, trims and packaging. More than 1,000 brands have trusted us with their production, a portfolio we think of as our most valuable patchwork project. We are also open to public tenders and institutional contracts for uniform shirts, from military and police to emergency and transport services.

Seventy people work in a factory of about 1,000 m², on Juki and Brother sewing lines, under an ISO 9001 quality management system. Between them they bring more than 30 years of collective experience in shirt construction and quality control.

Led by Women. Made by Women.

Garment work has long been done by women and rarely led by them. At EDES both are true: women run the company, and nine in ten of our 70 people are women, from the sewing lines to final inspection.

When you order from us, you support skilled women's work and a women-led business in Albania, and get shirts made by people who have spent years mastering them.

How We Work

Women-led, family-founded

Founded as a family business in 1998 and led by women today. We look for long-term partnerships, not one-off orders.

Close to our main market

Italy is our main market, a short distance from Tirana. Orders are collected from the factory or delivered to Durrës port, where your forwarder takes over.

An experienced team

70 people, 90% of them women, from machinists to inspectors, with more than 30 years of collective experience in shirt construction.

Always improving

ISO 9001 means documented processes we review and improve, alongside the hand skills that make a collar sit right.
